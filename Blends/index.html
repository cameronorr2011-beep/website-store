Why a blend

One cell size is one narrow ration

A single strain is a single size and a single nutritional profile. A blend spreads the sizes, so an animal whose mouthparts change next week still has something it can catch, and spreads the fats, because a golden cell and a diatom do not carry the same ones.

Questions

Before you send the order

The four things people ask about blends. Anything else: service@orrbiologicals.com.

Why is the blend quoted instead of priced?

Because it is mixed after the order. The cost depends on the size and what is growing well that week, and a fixed price on a live mix is a promise we would break. The reply gives you the price before anything is paid.

Can I order a blend with single strains?

Yes. Add both to the same cart. They ship together in one box, in the same Monday-to-Wednesday window, and the order arrives itemised.

How long does an opened bottle keep?

Two to three days, loosely capped, between roughly 15 and 20 °C and out of the light. Swirl it twice a day. Do not refrigerate it — cold and dark stops a culture rather than storing it.

Is a card included with a blend?

Yes, one card per blend, listing the strains in that batch, the size range and a starting feed rate. It is the same format as the single-strain cards, so the shelf stays consistent.
